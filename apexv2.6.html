Hardcoded Validation

The 7 Execution Gates

Even if all 3 AI agents hallucinate a BUY signal, the backend python engine physically blocks the trade unless it passes 7 rigorous mathematical checks.

Gate 1: Minimum Consensus

Combined AI score must be > 72%. If agents disagree (e.g., Macro holds, Scalper buys), the trade is instantly vetoed.

Gate 2: Killzone Timing

Trades are blocked outside of optimal London/NY overlap "Killzones". Low liquidity hours (Asian session) are heavily restricted.

Gate 3: HTF Trend Alignment

A SHORT trade is blocked if the 1H/4H EMA cascade is BULLISH. You cannot trade against the macro trend.

Gate 4: SMC Strike Zone

Price must be within 0.80% of a known institutional supply or demand level. No entering trades in the "middle of nowhere".

Gate 5: Early Entry Mode

Requires a first/second M15 resume candle after a retest. Blocks late entries when momentum is already over-extended.

Gate 6: Stack & Anti-Chase

Enforces max 1 position per symbol, applies a strict 45-minute cooldown after a closed trade, and prevents FOMO reentry.

Gate 7: Strict R:R Validation

The final hard gate. Every trade must have a Reward/Risk ratio >= 1.5. If the AI proposes an SL that ruins the ratio, the trade dies here.
